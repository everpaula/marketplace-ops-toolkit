Every planning cycle, the same fight. How many agents to hire, which vendor to keep, how much surge to run, which driver to coach. The loudest argument usually won, not the best math.

I kept rebuilding the same spreadsheets. Different company, different country, twelve years of it, the same five debates stitched together from scratch each time.

Built in a hurry, those models got cut in review or overbuilt in a panic. The math was usually right and always invisible, so the decision went political anyway.

So I pulled the math out of the spreadsheets, sanitized it, and turned each recurring decision into its own tool. A calculator or a simulator, single file, nothing to install.

Nine live tools now, across six ops verticals: fraud, supply chain, workforce planning, pricing, vendor management, driver performance. No login, no tracking, runs offline in a browser.

Every one of them does the same thing. It turns a recurring ops debate into a math problem instead of a political one.

Anywhere an ops decision deserves the actual numbers, not intuition.
