Landed Cost Calculator for Imported Kits

For teams that buy finish kits direct from factories in China and Vietnam: flooring, faucets, lighting, hardware. Put every quote side by side and rank it by landed cost in the US, not by FOB. Duty layers by country, containers filled by volume and weight, freight at $3k or $25k a box, margin per unit and the week your cash is most exposed.
Your project data stays in your browser. We collect nothing.

1Kit builder

One row per component of ONE apartment unit. Quantity is per unit, in the component's own unit of measure; waste covers cuts and attic stock. The HTS code and base duty are yours to set; this tool does not classify.

2Quotes by component

Two to four quotes per component. Price is per unit of measure at the incoterm shown. Cartons drive the container math, so carton volume and gross weight matter as much as price. Open the risk cell to tick the checks you have actually done.

Paste quotes from a spreadsheet

Copy rows from Excel or Google Sheets (tab separated) or CSV, with or without a header, in this column order: . The component name must match a row in step 1. New suppliers start with no checks ticked, so they rank as tier C until you verify them.

3Recommended mix, from your inputs

Per component, the lowest landed cost for the whole project among quotes at or above your tier floor, plus a second source. Picks and tiers come only from the prices and checks you entered, run through the formula in step 7. They are not a rating or endorsement of any supplier. Ranking uses full-container freight rates so one quote does not change another's number; the results below use your real container fill.

5Scenarios

Hit the recommended mix with a tariff change, a freight spike or a change of origin, and see it next to the base case. The contract price stays where the base case set it, so the margin takes the hit, as it would after signing.
