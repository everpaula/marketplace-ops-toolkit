How this works

This generator gives you the scaffold, not the content. Your priorities and context drive what shows up.

Each priority gets mapped into three phases:

  • Discover & Diagnose (first phase): map current state, interview stakeholders, baseline metrics.
  • Decide & Design (second phase): pick the approach, validate with pilot, commit to mechanism.
  • Deploy & Measure (third phase): ship the deliverable, establish cadence, track results.

Use the output as a starting structure. Edit the Markdown freely before sending. The point is to start from a shape that reads like an operating leader, not a template.
