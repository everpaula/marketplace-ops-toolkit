2. Estimates only. Do not rely on them alone

The tools produce estimates. They combine formulas, the information you enter, and default values such as tariff rates, customs fees, freight rates, container capacities and transit times. Each tool shows the date its defaults were researched.

  • Tariffs, trade rules, fees and freight rates change often, sometimes overnight and sometimes with retroactive effect. A default may be out of date, incomplete or wrong for your goods.
  • The formulas simplify. Real costs depend on facts the tools cannot see, such as your exact product, its classification, its country of origin, your contracts and your broker's charges.
  • Landed cost, duty, fees, margin, cash timeline, container fill and every other output is not a quote, a price, a ruling or a promise.
